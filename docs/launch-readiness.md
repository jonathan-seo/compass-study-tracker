# Launch readiness

Launch readiness turns the post-decision SOP into dated work on each study while leaving existing tracker records unchanged until a checklist is created.

## First implementation

- Open a study and choose **Create checklist** under Launch Readiness.
- Confirm the Planning Center page mode. `Information only` is the current study default.
- Confirm who is producing the page. `Hand off to Director of Admin` is the current default.
- Select only the applicable workflow sections, such as physical resources, digital material, offsite hosting, Sunday services, or a separate kickoff.
- Review calculated dates and override an individual checkpoint only when ministry reality requires it.
- Record status, owner, next action, blocker, and completion evidence as work progresses.
- Use the **Launch** view to work from blocked, overdue, and next-due items across studies.

Changing the study start date, duration, resource type, launch date, kickoff date, profile selection, or production path recalculates the applicable checklist while preserving checkpoint progress.

## Status meaning

- `Not started`, `In progress`, and `Waiting on owner` remain incomplete.
- `Blocked` remains incomplete and rises to the top of the Launch view.
- `Done` and `Not required` count as complete.
- `Accepted risk` remains incomplete so readiness cannot appear green merely because a risk was acknowledged.

## Planning Center rule

An information-only page still requires accurate publication and live verification. It does not require registration counts, capacity, payment, refunds, or a response deadline.

When Jonathan builds or materially corrects the page, the generated **Independent proof obtained** checkpoint must be completed. It does not appear on the Director of Admin handoff path.
