# Launch readiness

Launch readiness turns the post-decision SOP into dated work on each study while leaving existing tracker records unchanged until a checklist is created.

## First implementation

- Open a study and choose **Create checklist** under Launch Readiness.
- Confirm the Planning Center page mode. `Information only` is the current study default.
- Confirm who is producing the page. `Hand off to Director of Admin` is the current default.
- Select only the applicable workflow sections. Public information, leader acknowledgement, and a targeted invitation plan are core; Compass News, social media, and Sunday slides are optional broad channels chosen for the study.
- Treat a kickoff event as a separate linked event rather than a checkbox inside the study launch.
- When physical resources are used, set the actual distribution date so delivery readiness is calculated from the correct dependency.
- Review calculated dates and override an individual checkpoint only when ministry reality requires it.
- Record status, owner, next action, blocker, working notes, and completion evidence as work progresses.
- Use Working notes for evolving content such as master and tailored briefs, supplier communication, decisions, and stage-specific context. Keep Evidence or link for concise completion proof.
- Use the **Launch** view to work from blocked, overdue, and next-due items across studies.

Changing the study start date, duration, resource type, public launch date, resource distribution date, communication-channel selection, or production path recalculates the applicable checklist while preserving checkpoint progress.

## Status meaning

- `Not started`, `In progress`, and `Waiting on owner` remain incomplete.
- `Blocked` remains incomplete and rises to the top of the Launch view.
- `Done` counts as complete.
- `Not required` counts as complete only when the reason and confirming owner are recorded.
- `Accepted risk` counts toward `Ready with accepted risk` only when the decision owner, mitigation, and review date are recorded.

Changing a calculated target requires an override reason. The checklist also calls out missing owners and missing completion details.

## Planning Center rule

An information-only page still requires accurate publication and live verification. It does not require registration counts, capacity, payment, refunds, or a response deadline.

When Jonathan builds or materially corrects the page, the generated **Independent proof obtained** checkpoint must be completed. It does not appear on the Director of Admin handoff path.

Optional or required signup adds a verified signup/participation checkpoint. Paid registration also adds payment, refund, discount, and Finance-handoff verification. Selecting no public page adds an explicit exception and alternative-information checkpoint.

## Follow-up rhythm

- Complete an early operational check within two business days.
- Complete the leader health check seven to 14 days after the first session.
- Record closeout learning at the study or term end.
